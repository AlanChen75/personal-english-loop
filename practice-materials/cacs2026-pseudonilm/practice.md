# D03 先回憶再回答

只開此頁。關閉教材與核對頁。一次選一題，先用一句英文回答；需要時再加一句理由。教練在首次嘗試前，不先唸出答案或提示。答完後再開 [核對頁](answer-key.md)，修一個重點，關閉後重答。

## Typing prompts

先閉卷打字，再遮住你打的句子說一次。中文只提示溝通任務，不要求逐字翻譯。

| ID | 任務 |
|---|---|
| T01 | 用兩句英文介紹你的研究主題與目標。 |
| T02 | 用兩句話說明，為什麼不同工廠可能需要不同方法。 |
| T03 | 用三個短句說明系統輸入與選路方式。 |
| T04 | 解釋這個研究在哪些階段使用設備標籤。 |
| T05 | 先說主要結果，再補一句這些結果不能證明的事。 |
| T06 | 回覆一位想把方法直接用在新工廠的同事。 |
| T07 | 解釋 HIPE 的案例讓你注意到什麼問題。 |
| T08 | 先請對方把問題再說一次，再確認他問的是新工廠。 |

## Speaking prompts

先不看稿回答。目標是清楚表達，不追求每次相同措辭。

| ID | 場景與任務 |
|---|---|
| S01 | You meet a researcher at a conference. Introduce your research in about ten seconds. |
| S02 | A visitor asks, “Why is this problem difficult?” Give two short sentences. |
| S03 | A colleague asks, “How does your system work?” Explain the steps in about thirty seconds. |
| S04 | A chair asks, “What did you find?” Give a result and its limit. |
| S05 | A factory manager asks, “Can I use this at my plant now?” Give a careful answer. |
| S06 | Role-play a short Q&A. Ask the coach to repeat one question. Then answer and give one reason. |

## Retrieval Practice 核心題

1. **R01** What do you study?
2. **R02** What is the main question in your study?
3. **R03** Why might one method not fit every factory?
4. **R04** What does “stop” mean in this system?
5. **R05** What information does the system use?
6. **R06** What do the pseudo traces tell you?
7. **R07** How does the system choose a route?
8. **R08** When do you use equipment labels?
9. **R09** What is the main TECA result?
10. **R10** How should we understand the reference value in this study?
11. **R11** What can you say about event timing?
12. **R12** Why do you call this a pilot study?
13. **R13** What can you say about a new plant?
14. **R14** How would you answer “Is it fully label-free?”
15. **R15** How is HIPE used in this study?
16. **R16** What did you learn from the HIPE proxy test?

## Retrieval Practice 進階備用題

17. **R17** What is the TECA score for each scored dataset?
18. **R18** What happens when you remove each route-specific component?
19. **R19** What does the bootstrap interval describe?
20. **R20** What is the difference between a nameplate rating and an actual energy contribution?
21. **R21** What do the two HIPE audit or proxy scores refer to?
22. **R22** How can someone request WELTRON data?
23. **R23** What does the cost table include and exclude?
24. **R24** How does your study relate to other industrial NILM work?

## 回答後才做

用核對頁檢查意思與研究界線。再請教練改問一題；先別看變形題的核對提示。若卡住，請求一個 chunk 提示。隔日、第三天、第七天重抽曾卡住的題目。
