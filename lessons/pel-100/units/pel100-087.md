## 原文

Our customers pay a monthly fee to use the service. The price depends on how many devices they connect. This gives small companies a lower starting cost. As their needs grow, they can upgrade to a larger plan.

## T／L／E

- T 時態：現在式
- L 邏輯：收費方式→計價基礎→價值→擴展
- E 表達：depends on；as their needs grow
