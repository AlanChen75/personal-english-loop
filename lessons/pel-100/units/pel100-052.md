# 052｜處理團隊意見分歧（Handling Disagreement）

## 原文

We have different opinions about this design. I understand why you prefer the first option. However, I'm concerned about its long-term cost. Could we compare both options before making a decision?

## T／L／E

- T 時態：現在式
- L 邏輯：承認差異→理解→疑慮→比較
- E 表達：I'm concerned about；could we
