# 036｜睡眠與健康（Getting Enough Sleep）

## 原文

I didn't sleep well last night. I stayed up late to finish some work. As a result, I felt tired this morning. Tonight, I'll try to go to bed earlier.

- T 時態：過去式／未來式
- L 邏輯：原因→結果→改善
- E 表達：stay up late；as a result
