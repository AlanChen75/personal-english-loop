# 074｜城市與鄉村（City Life or Country Life）

- Stable unit ID: pel100-origin-074
- Lesson ID: pel100-074
- Origin ID: 074
- Source version: 1.1
- 階段：4（難度僅為設計意圖）

## 原文

Living in a city offers more job opportunities. Public transportation is also more convenient. On the other hand, the countryside is often quieter and less crowded. I'd prefer the city for work but the countryside for relaxation.

## T／L／E

- T 時態：現在式／偏好表達
- L 邏輯：城市優點→鄉村優點→個人選擇
- E 表達：on the other hand；I'd prefer
