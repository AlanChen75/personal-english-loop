# 056｜班機延誤（A Delayed Flight）

- Stable unit ID: pel100-origin-030
- Lesson ID: pel100-056
- Origin ID: 030

- 階段：3（難度僅為設計意圖）

## 原文

My flight has been delayed by two hours. I was supposed to arrive before dinner. Now I need to change my plans. I'll let my friends know as soon as I have a new arrival time.

## T／L／E

- T 時態：現在完成式／過去預期／未來式
- L 邏輯：變化→影響→應對
- E 表達：be supposed to；as soon as
