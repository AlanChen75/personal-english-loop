## 原文

Let's consider three possible outcomes. If demand grows quickly, we'll need more resources. If growth is slower than expected, we can control costs and improve the product. By preparing for both situations, we can make better decisions.

- T 時態：第一類條件句
- L 邏輯：情境→應對→風險管理
- E 表達：possible outcomes；slower than expected
