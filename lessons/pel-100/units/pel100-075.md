## 原文

I used to drive to work every day. Recently, I've started taking the train more often. It takes about the same amount of time, but I can read during the trip. As a result, my journey feels less stressful.

## T／L／E

- T 時態：used to／現在完成式
- L 邏輯：過去習慣→改變→比較→效果
- E 表達：used to；as a result
