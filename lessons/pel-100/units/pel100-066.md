# 066｜困難的選擇（Making a Difficult Decision）

## 原文

I once had to choose between two job opportunities. One offered more money, while the other offered better learning opportunities. I thought carefully about my long-term goals. Finally, I chose the job where I could learn more.

- T 時態：過去式
- L 邏輯：選項→比較→標準→決定
- E 表達：choose between；while
