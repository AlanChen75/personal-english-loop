# 088｜評估風險（Understanding Business Risks）

## 原文

Every new business faces uncertainty. One major risk is that customers may not be willing to pay. To reduce this risk, we plan to test the pricing before expanding. We'd rather learn early than spend too much money on the wrong idea.
