# 040｜錯過公車（Missing the Bus）

## 原文

I missed the bus this morning. I arrived at the stop just a minute too late. The next bus wasn't coming for another twenty minutes. So I decided to take a taxi instead.

## T／L／E

- T 時態：過去式／過去進行式
- L 邏輯：失誤→後果→替代方案
- E 表達：just a minute too late；instead
