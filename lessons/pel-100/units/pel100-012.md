## 原文

I need a ticket to the city center. Is there a direct train, or do I need to transfer? I'd prefer the fastest route. Could you tell me which platform to use?

## T／L／E

- T 時態：現在式
- L 邏輯：目的→路線比較→選擇
- E 表達：direct train；need to transfer
