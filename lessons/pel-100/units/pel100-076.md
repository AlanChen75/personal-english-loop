# 076｜消費與儲蓄（Spending and Saving）

## 原文

I try to save part of my income every month. Before buying something expensive, I ask myself whether I really need it. Sometimes waiting a few days helps me make a better decision. I've learned that careful spending is more important than buying things quickly.
