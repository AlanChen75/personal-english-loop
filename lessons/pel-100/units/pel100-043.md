# 043｜期限延後（Explaining a Delay）

## 原文

We may need two more days to finish the work. One of our suppliers delivered the materials late. As a result, we couldn't start testing on time. We're adjusting the schedule to reduce the delay.

## T／L／E

- T 時態：過去式／現在進行式
- L 邏輯：延期→原因→影響→對策
- E 表達：as a result；on time
