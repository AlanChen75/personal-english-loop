## 原文

Yesterday, I finished the first version of the report. Today, I'll review it with the team. I still need some data from the sales department. Once I get it, I can finish the analysis.

## T／L／E

- T 時態：過去式／未來式
- L 邏輯：昨日成果→今日安排→阻礙→條件
- E 表達：still need；once I get
