# 038｜我的家鄉（My Hometown）

## 原文

I grew up in a small town. It was quiet and had many beautiful places. Although I live in a big city now, I still miss my hometown. I visit it whenever I have time.

## T／L／E

- T 時態：過去式／現在式
- L 邏輯：過去→現在→情感
- E 表達：grew up；although；whenever

教材角色與情境不是使用者真實經歷；不改寫原文。先聽懂、跟讀，再用自己的情境重述。
