## 原文

I'm having trouble understanding this report. I've checked the numbers, but something doesn't look right. Could you review it with me? I think a second opinion would be helpful.

## T／L／E

- T 時態：現在進行式／現在完成式
- L 邏輯：困難→已採取行動→請求
- E 表達：having trouble；second opinion

教材角色與情境不是使用者真實經歷；不改寫原文。先聽懂、跟讀，再用自己的情境重述。
