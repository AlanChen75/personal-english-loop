## 原文

My internet connection stopped working this morning. I've already restarted the router twice. The problem is still there. Could you help me check whether there's a service outage?

## T／L／E

- T 時態：過去式／現在完成式
- L 邏輯：問題→已嘗試→現況→請求
- E 表達：restart；service outage
