## 原文

I used to believe that working longer meant getting more done. Then I noticed that tired people often make more mistakes. I started focusing on results instead of working hours. Now I believe working smarter is more useful than simply working longer.

## T／L／E

- T 時態：used to／過去式／現在式
- L 邏輯：原始信念→新證據→調整→新觀點
- E 表達：used to believe；instead of
