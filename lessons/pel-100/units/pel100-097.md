## 原文

We understand that the price is an important concern. However, we also need to maintain the quality of our service. If you can commit to a longer contract, we may be able to offer a discount. Would that be an acceptable option for your team?

## T／L／E

- T 時態：現在式／條件句
- L 邏輯：理解→限制→交換條件→確認
- E 表達：commit to；offer a discount
