# 007｜在咖啡店點餐（Ordering Coffee）

- Stable unit ID: pel100-origin-021

## 原文

I'd like a medium coffee, please. Could you make it without sugar? I'll have it to go because I'm in a hurry. How much is it altogether?

## T／L／E

- T 時態：現在式／禮貌請求
- L 邏輯：需求→條件→原因→詢價
- E 表達：I'd like；could you
