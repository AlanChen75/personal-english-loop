# Introducing Myself

## 今日目標

- 用 10 秒版本先敢開口。
- 依場合改用 30 秒或 60 秒版本。
- 回答六個常見的自我介紹問題。

## 10 seconds

Hello. I am Chung-Kuang Chen from Taiwan. My work is about AI and energy management.

## 30 seconds

Hello. I am Chung-Kuang Chen from Taiwan. My work is about AI, IoT, and energy management. I study power data from buildings and machines. I use the data to understand equipment behavior, find unusual changes, and help people save energy. I enjoy turning technical ideas into simple and useful tools.

## 60 seconds

Hello. I am Chung-Kuang Chen from Taiwan. I am from National Taiwan University of Science and Technology. My work brings together AI, IoT, and energy management. I study power signals from buildings and industrial machines. These signals help us understand how equipment works and when something changes. I use data to look for energy waste, unusual behavior, and possible problems. My goal is to make energy information easier to understand and use. In my research, I try to use clear methods and report limits honestly. I believe technology should support people, not replace their judgment. I also enjoy discussing research and learning from people in different fields. I am happy to share my work and hear about your ideas.

## Useful chunks

- I am ___ from Taiwan.
- My work is about ___.
- I study data from ___.
- I use the data to understand ___.
