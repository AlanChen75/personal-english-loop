Day one. What is energy management?

Hello. My work is about energy management. Energy management means using energy in a smart way. We first measure how much energy a building or a machine uses. Then we look at the data. We try to find waste, unusual changes, and ways to save energy.

For example, a machine may use too much power at night. The system can show this change. A manager can check the machine and take action.

The goal is simple. We want to use less energy, lower cost, and keep the equipment working well.
