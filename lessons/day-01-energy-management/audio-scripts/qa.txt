Day one. Question and answer practice. After each question, answer before you hear the model answer.

Question one. What do you work on?

Think and answer.

Model answer. I work on energy management.

Question two. What does energy management mean?

Think and answer.

Model answer. It means using energy in a smart way.

Question three. What do you measure?

Think and answer.

Model answer. We measure how much energy a building or a machine uses.

Question four. Why do you look at the data?

Think and answer.

Model answer. We want to find waste and unusual changes.

Question five. Can you give an example?

Think and answer.

Model answer. A machine may use too much power at night.

Question six. What can the system do?

Think and answer.

Model answer. The system can show the change.

Question seven. What can a manager do?

Think and answer.

Model answer. A manager can check the machine and take action.

Question eight. What is your goal?

Think and answer.

Model answer. We want to save energy, lower cost, and keep equipment working well.
