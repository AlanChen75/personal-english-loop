# Day 1 — What is Energy Management?

## 今日目標

練完後，你可以用簡單英文說明：

- 什麼是能源管理。
- 為什麼要看能源資料。
- 你的工作或研究如何幫助使用者節省能源。

## Core Transcript

### Mini talk

Hello. My work is about energy management.

Energy management means using energy in a smart way. We first measure how much energy a building or a machine uses. Then we look at the data. We try to find waste, unusual changes, and ways to save energy.

For example, a machine may use too much power at night. The system can show this change. A manager can check the machine and take action.

The goal is simple. We want to use less energy, lower cost, and keep the equipment working well.

### Short dialogue

**Alex:** What do you work on?

**Alan:** I work on energy management.

**Alex:** What does that mean?

**Alan:** We measure energy use and look at the data.

**Alex:** Why do you do that?

**Alan:** We want to find waste and help people save energy.

**Alex:** Can you give me an example?

**Alan:** Yes. If a machine uses too much power, the system can show it.

**Alex:** What happens next?

**Alan:** A manager can check the machine and take action.

## Three answer lengths

### 10 seconds

I work on energy management. We use data to find waste and help people save energy.

### 30 seconds

My work is about energy management. We measure how much energy a building or a machine uses. Then we look at the data and find unusual changes. The goal is to save energy, lower cost, and keep equipment working well.

### 60 seconds

Hello. My work is about energy management. Energy management means using energy in a smart way. We first measure the energy use of a building or a machine. Then we look at the data. We try to find waste and unusual changes. For example, a machine may use too much power at night. The system can show this, so a manager can check the machine and take action. Our goal is to save energy, lower cost, and keep the equipment working well.

## Useful Chunks

| Chunk | 中文意思 | 替換練習 |
|---|---|---|
| My work is about ___ . | 我的工作與⋯⋯有關 | energy / AI / data |
| We measure ___ . | 我們測量⋯⋯ | energy use / power / temperature |
| We look at the data. | 我們查看資料 | — |
| We try to find ___ . | 我們試著找出⋯⋯ | waste / changes / problems |
| The system can show ___ . | 系統可以顯示⋯⋯ | the change / the problem / the result |
| We help people ___ . | 我們幫助人們⋯⋯ | save energy / lower cost / take action |
| The goal is simple. | 目標很簡單 | — |

## Vocabulary and Root Hints

| Word | Simple meaning | Root hint |
|---|---|---|
| energy | power used to do work | Greek *en* (in) + *ergon* (work): work within |
| manage | plan and control something | Think: manage → manager → management |
| measure | find the size or amount | Same family: measure, measurement |
| unusual | not normal | `un-` means “not” + usual |
| action | something you do | `act` means “do”; act → action → active |
| equipment | tools or machines for work | `equip` means “give what is needed” |

字根只用來幫助記憶，不要求背誦。今天真正要會說的是 chunks。

## Q&A Practice

先用一句回答。熟悉後再用兩到三句。

1. What do you work on?
   - I work on energy management.
2. What does energy management mean?
   - It means using energy in a smart way.
3. What do you measure?
   - We measure how much energy a building or a machine uses.
4. Why do you look at the data?
   - We want to find waste and unusual changes.
5. Can you give an example?
   - A machine may use too much power at night.
6. What can the system do?
   - The system can show the change.
7. What can a manager do?
   - A manager can check the machine and take action.
8. What is your goal?
   - We want to save energy, lower cost, and keep equipment working well.
