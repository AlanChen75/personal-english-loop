# Day 2 — How AI and IoT Help Energy Management

## 今日目標

練完後，你可以用簡單英文說明：

- IoT 如何收集資料。
- AI 如何從資料找出變化。
- 人仍然如何做最後決定。

## Core Transcript

### Mini talk

Today I want to explain how AI and IoT help energy management.

IoT devices collect data from buildings and machines. They can measure power, temperature, and operating time. The data tells us what is happening now.

AI helps us look for patterns in the data. It can find a change that is hard to see. It can also give an early warning when something may be wrong.

AI does not make every decision. It gives useful information to people. A manager or an engineer checks the situation and decides what to do.

In short, IoT collects the data, AI helps us understand it, and people take action.

## Vocabulary and Root Hints

| Word | Simple meaning | Root hint |
|---|---|---|
| collect | bring things together | `col-` means “together”; collect → collection |
| pattern | something that happens in a regular way | Think: a repeated shape or change |
| operating | working or running | `oper` means “work”; operate → operation |
| information | facts that help us understand | `form` means “shape”; information gives shape to an idea |
| decision | a choice after thinking | decide → decision |
| engineer | a person who designs or works with systems | Keep it as one useful professional word |
| warning | a sign of possible danger or trouble | warn → warning |

字根只用來幫助記憶。今天先熟練 `collect data`、`look for patterns`、`give a warning`、`take action`。
