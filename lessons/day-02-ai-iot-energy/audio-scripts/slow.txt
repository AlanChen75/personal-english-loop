Day two. How AI and IoT help energy management.

Listen slowly.

Today I want to explain how AI and IoT help energy management.

IoT devices collect data from buildings and machines.

They can measure power, temperature, and operating time.

The data tells us what is happening now.

AI helps us look for patterns in the data.

It can find a change that is hard to see.

It can also give an early warning when something may be wrong.

AI does not make every decision.

It gives useful information to people.

A manager or an engineer checks the situation and decides what to do.

In short, IoT collects the data, AI helps us understand it, and people take action.
