We also asked whether wide-range proposals satisfy all declared active engineering limits. Only 5 out of 225 unbounded LLM joint configurations did so: 2.22%. For LHS, it was 54 out of 900, or 6%. The LLM proposals had a smaller average normalized violation distance, so they tended to lie closer to the ranges, but that did not translate into reliable full-configuration compliance. Neither source's selected unbounded configurations were fully compliant in this audit. “Close to a bound” and “satisfies every bound” are different claims. These feasibility figures come from an exploratory validation-only audit, not from additional sealed-test evaluations.
