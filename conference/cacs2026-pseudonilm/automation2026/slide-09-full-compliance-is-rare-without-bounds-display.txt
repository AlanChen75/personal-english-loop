This is an exploratory validation-only audit. We checked whether each wide-range joint configuration met every active engineering limit. Only 5 out of 225 LLM configurations did so, or 2.22%. For LHS, 54 out of 900 did so, or 6%. The LLM proposals were closer to the ranges on average, but fewer met all the limits at once. Also, none of the selected unbounded configurations from either source were fully compliant. Being close to a range does not mean meeting every limit. These figures describe candidate feasibility. They add no new sealed-test result.
