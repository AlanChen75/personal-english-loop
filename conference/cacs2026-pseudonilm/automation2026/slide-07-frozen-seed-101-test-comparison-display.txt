In the frozen seed 101 test comparison, LHS rose from 0.0991 without bounds to 0.6244 with bounds. The LLM arm rose from 0.4850 to 0.5577. Those are differences between complete bounded and unbounded procedures, not isolated effects of the candidate limits. The strongest pooled result was actually a simple threshold-only baseline, at 0.8059 MCC. This is important to state plainly: our more elaborate candidate generators did not win the overall test comparison. The observed gain in the bounded arms supports examining the role of engineering limits, while the baseline keeps the performance claim in perspective.
