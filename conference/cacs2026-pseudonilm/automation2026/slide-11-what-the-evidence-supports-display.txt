The evidence says that candidate feasibility and selection quality must be checked before claiming value from a more complex generator. On these data, the unbounded LLM pools had a selected validation advantage, but no bounded LLM advantage was detected. A simple threshold baseline remained strongest in the frozen pooled test result. We cannot say that the LLM learned physical laws, that bounds alone caused a gain, or that the method is ready for another facility. The bounds affect both generation and reconstruction, the two generators receive different information, and the repeated-test evidence is asymmetric. Those limits determine what the next experiment needs to isolate.
