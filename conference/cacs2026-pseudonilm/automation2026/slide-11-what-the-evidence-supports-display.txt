The three rows summarize three evidence sets. In validation, unbounded LLM pools had higher selected MCC, but we found no bounded LLM advantage. In the frozen seed 101 test, the threshold-only baseline remained strongest. Across 5 LLM test seeds, bounds reduced variation but also lowered mean MCC. Together, these findings show why we need to check candidate feasibility, selection quality, and a simple baseline. Bounds change both generation and reconstruction. The generators receive different information, and repeated-test evidence is unequal. We cannot isolate the effect of bounds alone or claim that the method will generalize to another facility.
