Industrial nil-em estimates individual device states from an aggregate power trace. In a new site, device-level labels may be unavailable during calibration. We therefore have to select parameters using aggregate data alone. Those parameters control such things as power thresholds and the reconstruction of each device's signal. A configuration that looks plausible by engineering intuition can still perform poorly on labeled device states. This makes candidate generation and candidate selection separate problems. Our experiment keeps the selection rule fixed and compares two ways to supply it with candidate configurations.
