Here, a bound is a prescribed engineering range for a calibration parameter, such as a power factor or an on-ratio. In the bounded sampling arm, Latin hypercube sampling draws directly within the declared interval. It does not draw a wide value and then clip or reject it. The unbounded arm keeps its wider-range candidates. Bounds also restrict accepted events and cap reconstructed device power. So enabling bounds changes both the candidate domain and the output reconstruction rule. If performance changes, this experiment cannot assign the change to either part alone. Also, our declared rectangular ranges are not a measured ground-truth feasible region.
