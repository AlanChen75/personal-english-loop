We used 3 industrial corpora: IMDELD, Case 1, and WELTRON, with 9 devices in total. Each trace was aligned to 1 power sample per second and divided chronologically into calibration, validation, and test partitions, in a 50%, 20%, 30% split. We crossed two candidate sources, one-shot LLM proposals and Latin hypercube sampling, with bounds enabled or disabled. For each dataset, source, bounds condition, and seed, the pool contained 15 joint configurations. The LLM's device proposals were aligned by index; we did not form every possible combination. Both sources received the same candidate-count and calibration-sample budgets.
