We used 3 industrial corpora: IMDELD, Case 1, and WELTRON, with 9 devices in total. Each trace was aligned to 1 power sample per second. We used a chronological 50%, 20%, 30% split for calibration, validation, and test. The table shows four conditions: LLM wide, LLM bounded, LHS wide, and LHS bounded. LHS means Latin hypercube sampling. Each pool contained 15 joint configurations for each dataset, source, bounds condition, and seed. For the LLM, device proposals were joined by index, rather than by every possible combination. Both sources received the same candidate-count and calibration-sample budgets.
