We used three industrial corpora: eye em dee ee el dee, Case one, and well-tron, with nine devices in total. Each trace was aligned to one power sample per second and divided chronologically into calibration, validation, and test partitions, in a fifty, twenty, thirty percent split. We crossed two candidate sources, one-shot ell ell em proposals and Latin hypercube sampling, with bounds enabled or disabled. For each dataset, source, bounds condition, and seed, the pool contained fifteen joint configurations. The ell ell em's device proposals were aligned by index; we did not form every possible combination. Both sources received the same candidate-count and calibration-sample budgets.
