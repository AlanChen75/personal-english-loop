To understand selection, we examined existing validation pools after the main experiment. In the unbounded condition, selected LLM configurations exceeded selected LHS configurations by 0.3150 MCC when devices were weighted equally. When datasets were weighted equally, the difference was 0.2786. We separated two ingredients: the best validation MCC available inside each fixed pool, which is an oracle ceiling, and the gap between that ceiling and what our aggregate-only scorer selected, which is selection regret. Selected performance equals ceiling minus regret. The device-balanced decomposition suggested differences in both availability and selection; under dataset weighting, the oracle difference was close to zero and the regret difference dominated. These are post-hoc validation diagnostics. The oracle sees device labels and is not a deployable selector.
