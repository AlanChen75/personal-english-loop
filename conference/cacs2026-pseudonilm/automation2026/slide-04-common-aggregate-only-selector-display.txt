First, we form the LLM and LHS candidate pools. The same selector then ranks every pool using aggregate data only. The formula on the right combines residual fit, event agreement, state consistency, and separation. Their weights are 0.35, 0.30, 0.20, and 0.15. No device-level labels enter selection. We freeze the selected configuration first, and only then evaluate it with test labels. Candidate-count and calibration-sample budgets are equal. Generation information differs: the LLM also sees device information, aggregate statistics, and the scorer description. LHS uses the parameter ranges and a seed. Generation time and cost are not matched.
