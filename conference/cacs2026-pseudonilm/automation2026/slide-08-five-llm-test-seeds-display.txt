A single seed can be misleading, so we also have 5 LLM generation seeds with sealed-test results. Without bounds, mean MCC was 0.6262, with a sample standard deviation of 0.1131. With bounds, the mean was 0.5951, with a smaller standard deviation of 0.0413. In this 5-seed set, bounds reduced dispersion but did not increase mean performance. The LHS test result on the previous slide comes from 1 fixed seed. Its 20-seed repetition was examined only on validation data. We therefore should not describe the 5-seed LLM test comparison as a matched repeated-test contest against LHS.
