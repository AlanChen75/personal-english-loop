The four corpora serve different roles. IMDELD is an open industrial-machine dataset with six scored devices. WELTRON is a private field dataset with two scored targets. Case1 is a single-compressor case in IEEE DataPort. These three receive attribution scores. HIPE is public and useful, but it is used only to examine the no-score boundary. It is not a fourth scored result. This distinction matters because all four corpora informed the pilot protocol.
