Recent industrial NILM studies address different parts of this problem. Li and colleagues use equipment and process constraints in a mixed-integer model. Fabri and colleagues reduce labeling demand through active learning, although a supervised stage remains. Yan and colleagues show that differences in datasets, metrics, and implementations still make NILM results difficult to compare. Our study targets a different operating condition. We freeze a route-selection protocol, apply bounded transformations, and allow an explicit no-score result. We do not claim state-of-the-art superiority over these methods.
