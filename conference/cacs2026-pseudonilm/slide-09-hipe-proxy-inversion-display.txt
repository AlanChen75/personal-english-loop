The most important negative result appears in HIPE. The retrospective equipment-based audit gives HIPE a score of 0.1602, which is consistent with abstention. A nameplate-only proxy raises the score to 0.9220 and changes the decision to score. The reason is that monitored devices contribute only 3.83% of aggregate energy. Nameplate ratings describe device potential, but they do not measure realized contribution to the observed aggregate. This result shows that a simple nameplate proxy is unsafe for this boundary. A deployable identifiability proxy remains unresolved.
