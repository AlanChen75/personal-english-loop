The evaluation has five stages. Development establishes the candidate routes. Validation labels select a small set of bounded constants. We then freeze the protocol. During the reported execution, the system reads only aggregate power, device priors, and pseudo traces. Equipment labels are used afterward for tee ee see ay and descriptive diagnostics. This separation prevents labels from entering dispatch or trace construction in the reported final execution. However, all four corpora informed the design, and earlier diagnostics affected component development. We therefore describe the evidence as a traceable pilot, not as a pristine confirmatory test.
