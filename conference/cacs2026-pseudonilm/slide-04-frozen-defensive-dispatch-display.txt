The system receives aggregate power, declared device priors, and deterministic pseudo traces. These pseudo traces contain timing evidence but no target labels. A frozen dispatch rule selects one of four outcomes. IMDELD uses modulated allocation with pair calibration. WELTRON uses guarded repair. Case1 uses event rectangles. HIPE receives no score because its evaluated configuration does not provide the required pseudo-trace support. Equipment labels enter only after reconstruction, when we calculate TECA and descriptive diagnostics. The design principle is simple: do not force every corpus through the same reconstruction path.
