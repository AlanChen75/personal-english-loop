# Phase 1 Curriculum

第一階段共 30 個情境。排序固定為 D → E → B → C → F → A；每類五課。

## D — Professional English

1. What is energy management?
2. How AI and IoT help energy management
3. Why we analyze power signals
4. Understanding equipment behavior
5. Finding waste and improving efficiency

## E — Academic English

1. My research question
2. Why this problem matters
3. The data I use
4. My research method
5. Explaining results and limits

## B — About Me

1. Introducing myself
2. My professional background
3. What I study
4. What I am working on now
5. My goals and interests

## C — Work and Projects

1. Introducing my company or team
2. Explaining a project
3. Giving a progress update
4. Explaining a problem
5. Suggesting a solution

## F — Discussion English

1. Benefits and risks of AI
2. Energy saving and daily life
3. Smart buildings and smart factories
4. Data privacy and connected devices
5. The future of work and technology

## A — Daily Life

1. Meeting someone for the first time
2. Ordering food and coffee
3. Asking for directions
4. Hotel and travel
5. Small talk

## 每課固定組件

- Core transcript
- Slow audio
- Natural audio
- Listen-and-repeat audio
- Q&A audio
- Useful chunks
- Vocabulary and root hints
- ChatGPT voice practice
- Progress record
